Cyber-Physical Systems: programme structure and course pool

The supplied 45-page programme document describes an 80-credit M.Tech curriculum. Its course pool includes CPS architecture, control-system design, cyber security of physical systems, IoT, intelligent and robust control, stochastic processes, digital control, robotics, dynamical-system simulation, sensors and interfacing, data communication and networking, DSP, mechatronics, system identification, adaptive and model-predictive control, nonlinear control, network security, embedded systems, multi-agent systems, digital twins, edge/cloud computing and fault detection.

The curriculum also lists laboratory exposure to Python/MATLAB modelling, Raspberry Pi, Arduino and sensors, FPGA applications, mobile-robot multi-agent systems and LoRa communication. This is presented as programme curriculum, not as a claim that every course or laboratory was completed.
