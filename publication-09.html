01 / RESEARCH OVERVIEW

Research scope

This work addresses underwater-image degradation—low contrast, colour distortion, haze and scattering—that makes visual identification of metal surfaces difficult. The proposed approach combines transform-based denoising/enhancement with morphological processing for metal selection.

02 / TECHNICAL APPROACH

Methodology described in the paper

The manuscript describes DCT and wavelet-transform techniques for denoising and enhancement, alongside morphological operations such as dilation and erosion to refine boundaries and suppress small artefacts. The goal is clearer identification of corroded and non-corroded metal regions.
