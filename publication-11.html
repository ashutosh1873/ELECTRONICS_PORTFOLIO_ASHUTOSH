SOURCE / PAPER ABSTRACT

Abstract

In this study, we propose a hybrid spam detection model using a voting classifier that combines Extra Trees Classifier, Multinomial Naive Bayes (Multinomial NB), and Support Vector Classifier (SVC). By leveraging the strengths of each individual model, the proposed approach aims to achieve higher accuracy and robustness in distinguishing spam from legitimate messages. We evaluate the model on standard datasets and compare its performance to traditional single-classifier models. Experimental results show that our voting classifier significantly improves spam detection metrics such as accuracy, precision, recall, and F1-score
